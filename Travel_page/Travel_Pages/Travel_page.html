Travel Glossary:

Before you book your trip, here are some common travel terms to help you understand our packages better.

1. Visa
An official document or stamp that allows entry into a foreign country.
2. Travel Insurance
Protection that covers unexpected events like illness, trip cancellation, or lost luggage.
3. Peak Season
The time of year when a destination is most crowded and prices are highest.
4. Off-Season
The less busy time of year when travel is usually cheaper.
